# EPIC-260004: Upfront plan authoring

> **Parked 2026-09-21** by the owner; status unchanged (`Ideation`). Until it resumes, the plan is
> authored by editing the content files of [ADR-260008](../adr/ADR-260008.md) — the same vocabulary
> this epic would put behind a screen. **Resumes** when plan authoring moves into the app.

- Epic ID: EPIC-260004
- Title: Upfront plan authoring
- Persona/Actor: The owner planning a training block away from the gym, with time to think and both
  hands free.
- Value statement: For the owner, deliver a way to write down which exercises, how many sets and how
  many reps belong to each planned day, so that the gym screen has something to show.
- Context: The owner's framing is that the workout is planned upfront and the gym screen only reads
  it. A plan is small — a handful of days of 5-7 exercises — so it is one of the few Liftrack reads
  that sits comfortably inside the 25-atom cap today, and the exercise catalog it points at is one
  of the few that does not.
- Problem statement: Nothing can be shown at the gym until a plan exists, and no plan can exist
  until its structure is agreed. Two authoring questions are distinct and must not be conflated:
  the **exercise catalogue** is settled — REQ-FR-260002 requires the user to create and edit it
  in-product, no seeding — but whether the **plan/routine** itself is authored in the MVP at all,
  or whether the first plan is seeded some other way, is still genuinely open (bullet 1 below).
- Expected outcome: a plan exists in the backend in the shape ADR-260001 ratifies, readable by the
  gym screen, its exercises drawn from a catalogue the user built themselves.
- Success metric(s): the owner can produce a complete week of planned training unaided; a plan and
  its days read back in at most two calls, each returning far fewer than 25 atoms; editing a plan
  leaves every already-logged session's recorded prescription unchanged.
- Non-goals: coaching intelligence, automatic progression or periodisation maths; sharing a plan
  with anyone; exercise media or a video library; importing from third-party training apps.
- Constraints: the atom shape from ADR-260001; reference cells create no bonds and are not expanded
  on a plain read, so each plan-to-exercise link costs a round trip to resolve; `retrieve` cannot
  fetch a set of uuids in one call, and its only batch-shaped argument is on the destroy path.
- Dependencies: ADR-260001 (**Accepted** 2026-09-12) and the Liftrack atom shape contract reaching
  `Active` (still `Proposed`, pending its own O1/O2 live-probe items); a user-authored exercise
  catalogue (REQ-FR-260002) to point at, whose own read already exceeds the 25-atom cap for any
  serious program.
- Risks/assumptions: assumes plans change rarely, which is what makes whole-plan rewrites
  acceptable. Plan-versus-actual comparison is **not** an open assumption — it was considered and
  explicitly dropped from the baseline shape (`liftrack-atom-shape-contract.md` §6, "Not an
  invariant of this baseline") and lives only as a deferred roadmap candidate; do not reopen it here
  without reopening it there first.
- Target window: TBD.
- Validation approach: end-to-end authoring of one plan, read back by the gym screen; a contract
  check that a planned-day payload validates against the atom shape contract; an assertion that
  editing a plan leaves an existing session's recorded prescription untouched.

## Ideation Bullets

Three of the original six bullets are resolved by ADR-260001 (Accepted 2026-09-12) and
REQ-FR-260002 as ratified; struck through and left visible rather than deleted, per this
project's practice of showing what closed a question rather than erasing it.

- [ ] **Is plan authoring in the MVP, or are the first plans seeded another way?** Still open, and
  distinct from the catalogue question below. Seeding — a one-off scripted write, or hand-authored
  atoms — gets set logging to the gym sooner and defers the largest editing surface in the product.
  The cost is that the owner cannot change a plan unaided, which is a real limitation for a product
  whose whole premise is that plans are made upfront.
- [x] ~~Plan as several atoms, or as one document atom?~~ **Resolved by ADR-260001**: separate
  `LiftPlan` + `LiftPlanDay` atoms (Option C, the single document atom, was considered and
  rejected in the ADR's Alternatives Considered — it forecloses per-day addressing).
- [ ] **Calendar days or a rotation?** Still open. "Monday is Push" and "next day in the cycle"
  give different answers after a missed session, and the choice defines what EPIC-260005's screen
  means by today.
- [ ] **What happens when a plan is edited mid-programme?** Still open, but narrower than
  originally framed: plan-versus-actual snapshotting is no longer one of the options (dropped from
  the baseline shape, see EPIC-260004's Risks/assumptions above), so the real question is only
  whether an edit made mid-programme should retroactively change how an *already-logged* session
  is displayed, or never touch it. REQ-FR-260002's acceptance criteria already require the latter
  for sessions logged before the edit; this bullet is about sessions logged after it.
- [x] ~~Where does the exercise catalog come from, and who owns it?~~ **Resolved by REQ-FR-260002**
  (ratified alongside ADR-260001): user-authored in-product, not seeded. The catalogue-read-exceeds-
  25 concern this bullet raised is real and unresolved, but it is a *different* question, already
  tracked honestly as an accepted risk in `liftrack-atom-shape-contract.md` §5 (no taxonomy
  workaround) and REQ-CR-260003 (truncation is detected, not designed away).
- [x] ~~How precise is a prescription?~~ **Resolved**: REQ-FR-260002 and `planday/v1`
  (`liftrack-atom-shape-contract.md` §3.1) fix it as a planned set count plus a target rep count or
  rep range. A target weight, a percentage of a maximum, and free text were each considered and are
  explicitly *not* in the baseline row shape — reopening any of them is a `planday/v2` column, a
  contract amendment, not a silent addition.
